# Tower Toss

Build a wonder, one well-timed toss at a time.

Tower Toss is a timing and building game about a quarry crew with a big job. Hold to charge, release to throw, and send each stone towards the worker on the ledge above. Find the right power, read the carrier's lean and keep the block moving towards the surface.

A careful catch keeps your run alive. A precise throw can skip a ledge and grow your combo. Miss the mark and your crew may have a very different kind of catch to deal with.

## From quarry to construction site
Every delivered stone contributes to your monument. Take control of the crane, swing the block into position and time its release. Place bricks and watch your build take shape, section by section. Clean construction placements earn stars.

## Twelve monuments to discover
Start at Foundation Camp and work towards the Arctic Igloo, Stonehenge, Pompeii Forum, Angkor Sanctuary and Chichen Itza. Continue through the Amber Ziggurat, Rune Torii, Jade Pagoda, Ruby Stupa and Emerald Palace to the Diamond Crown.

## Make your crew your own
Earn coins through play and choose outfits for your workers. Discover 20 stone materials, from sandstone and slate to rune stones, jade, ruby, emerald and diamond. Outfit choices keep the same throwing physics, so style stays a personal choice.

## A little help for a difficult toss
Use Steady Hands to settle a worker, Slow Charge to give yourself more time on the power meter, or Second Life to save a run when the last heart is lost. Fly lets you hover and boost a block during its throw.

## A builder's journey
Pursue staged bronze, silver, gold and platinum trophies. Improve your scores, discover stones and complete monuments. Review saved runs in the local leaderboard and keep working towards your next milestone.
